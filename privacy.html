Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

FitFore ("we", "us") is a fitness, nutrition and golf training app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • What you log: food and calories, water, workouts and lifts, golf practice and rounds, recovery, weekly weigh-ins, notes, progress photos, and the goals and body details you enter (such as age, height, weight and sex) to work out calorie targets.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and request times) to run and protect the service.

We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

  • To run the app: save your logs, show your progress and keep you signed in.
  • For AI features: when you tap Estimate calories, Coach me, Build my plan or ask for form tips, the text you typed for that request is sent to Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, to generate the answer. Anthropic processes it to provide the service under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models by default.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

Who stores it

Your account and logs are stored with Supabase. The app is hosted on Vercel. Both act as service providers on our behalf. Your data is private to your account: database rules stop other users from reading it.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: on the Goals tab, tap Delete all my data. Your logs, photos and settings are deleted right away.
  • Delete your account: email us at fitforetraining@gmail.com from your account's email address and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Get a copy: email us and we will send you a copy of your data.

Health information

Some of what you log, like weight and body details, is personal. We use it only to provide FitFore to you. FitFore is not a medical service and is not covered by HIPAA.

Children

FitFore is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
